The working island. This strip carries .dark-bg, the only class selector in the dark block that matches anything. It should flip to charcoal with white text while everything around it stays white.

Our local history collection has finished being catalogued. Around four hundred items that were previously request-only are now on open shelves at the back of the ground floor.
