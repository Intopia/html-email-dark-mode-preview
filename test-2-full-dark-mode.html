Riverbend Library

September notes for members

The upstairs reading room reopens on the 22nd after six weeks of rewiring. The long tables are back, the window seats are back, and the radiator that everybody complained about has been replaced with something quieter.

Our local history collection has finished being catalogued. Around four hundred items that were previously request-only are now on open shelves at the back of the ground floor.

Renewals can be done at the desk or over the phone. If your card expired during the closure, we have already extended it by two months, so there is nothing you need to do.
