HTML email dark mode preview

Shows what dark-mode support an HTML email declares, and what the email's own dark-mode CSS actually produces when it is switched on. This is a viewer, not a test. Nothing here passes or fails, and it makes no claim about how any particular email client will render the email.

Nothing you paste or upload here is sent anywhere. This preview runs entirely in your browser.

About this preview

Scripts are blocked in the preview, which matches every email client. Remote images will load, so tracking pixels in a real email may fire.

The dark view switches on the email's own @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) rules in place, leaving them exactly where the author put them in the cascade. No rules are rewritten, moved or invented.
