About this preview

Scripts are blocked in the preview, which matches every email client. Remote images will load, so tracking pixels in a real client's email may fire.

The dark view switches on the email's own @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) rules in place, leaving them exactly where the author put them in the cascade. No rules are rewritten, moved or invented.
